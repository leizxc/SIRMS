THE RESPONSE PATH

Five steps from report to readiness.

Every stage gives teams a clear next action and keeps incident follow-up connected to equipment safety.

01 REPORT

Report an Incident

Employees and witnesses submit the incident details, location, and supporting evidence.

Start with a clear, timely report →
02 TRIAGE

Review & Validate

Safety triage confirms urgency, hazards, and the appropriate response priority.

03 CONTAIN

Link & Quarantine Equipment

Attach a Tool ID or serial number to the report and flag defective equipment for quarantine or maintenance.

04 CORRECT

Investigate & Correct

Inspect the event, document root causes, and record actions to prevent a repeat incident.

05 CLEAR

Monitor Resolution & Readiness

Follow incident closure and equipment status until the tool is safely cleared for use.
