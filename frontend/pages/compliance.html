COMPLIANCE SUPPORT

Organize the work behind a stronger safety program.

SafeSite helps teams document, review, and coordinate safety activities. It does not itself certify legal or regulatory compliance.

01

OSHA Standard Logs

Organize incident information for internal review and follow-up.

Current scope: Official OSHA recordability decisions and required Forms 300, 301, and 300A remain the responsibility of a qualified safety administrator.

02

ISO 45001 Framework

Support parts of an occupational health and safety management workflow.

Current scope: SafeSite does not replace a complete ISO 45001 management system, audit program, or certification process.

03

Incident Taxonomy

Use consistent categories to improve routing and trend review.

Current scope: Teams should define and maintain their approved incident taxonomy and records policy.

04

Data Privacy & GDPR

Apply role-aware access controls to help protect the portal.

Current scope: Privacy notices, lawful processing decisions, retention procedures, and security reviews remain organization responsibilities.
