HOW IT WORKS

From Report to Resolution

A simple, transparent, and structured 5-step lifecycle ensuring every incident is handled promptly with zero gaps.

✎1

Report an Incident

Employee or witness submits details, location, and photos via portal or mobile.

✔2

Review & Validation

Safety triage validates urgency, hazards, and initiates priority queue assignment.

🔍3

Investigation

Assigned safety officers inspect on-site, interview staff, and gather proof logs.

📋4

Action & Findings

Root causes and permanent corrective action plans are documented & enforced.

📊5

Status Monitoring

All stakeholders monitor live status, compliance analytics, and final closure signs.
