HOW IT WORKS

From Report to Resolution

A simple, transparent, and structured 5-step lifecycle that keeps every incident and affected tool moving toward safe resolution.

STEP 01

Report an Incident

Employee or witness submits details, location, and photos via portal or mobile.

STEP 02

Review & Validation

Safety triage validates urgency, hazards, and initiates priority queue assignment.

STEP 03

Link & Quarantine Equipment

Tag the affected Tool ID or serial number, then flag defective equipment for quarantine or maintenance.

STEP 04

Investigate & Correct

Assigned safety officers inspect the worksite, document root causes, and record corrective action plans.

STEP 05

Monitor Resolution & Readiness

Track incident closure and equipment status until the tool is cleared for safe use.

COMPLIANCE SUPPORT

How SafeSite Supports Your Safety Program

See which workflows SafeSite can help organize today, and where your organization still needs formal procedures, review, or additional system features.

Scope: SafeSite helps document and coordinate safety work. It does not certify an organization or automatically determine legal compliance.

01

OSHA Standard Logs

Organize incident information for review and follow-up.

What SafeSite supports

  • The reporter portal displays a five-step incident reporting guide, including category, location, description, and evidence steps.
  • Reporter and officer pages show example report statuses and response stages for workflow reference.
  • Plant sector records can store site names, addresses, risk tiers, and facility leads for context.

Current scope: Incident examples and workflow screens are not yet connected to a persistent incident API, and SafeSite does not generate OSHA Forms 300, 301, or 300A. A qualified safety administrator must decide recordability and maintain required official logs.

02

ISO 45001 Framework

Support parts of an occupational health and safety management workflow.

What SafeSite supports

  • The working plant sector module stores operating locations, risk tiers, facility leads, and active status in the database.
  • Reporter guidance outlines reporting, categorization, description, evidence, and status tracking stages.
  • Separate reporter, officer, and administrator views communicate role-specific workflows.

Current scope: These tools do not implement the full ISO 45001 management system, internal audit program, worker consultation records, or certification process. Your organization must maintain those processes and evidence separately.

03

Incident Taxonomy

Use clear categories to make incident reports easier to review consistently.

What SafeSite supports

  • The reporter guidance presents example hazard categories such as chemical, slip/trip, structural, and equipment.
  • Consistent labels can help teams route reports and review recurring types of hazards.
  • Plant sector risk tiers provide additional site context for safety review.

Current scope: The category examples are guidance in the interface; there is not yet a centrally managed taxonomy saved with persistent incident records. Administrators should define and maintain the categories used by their organization.

04

Data Privacy & GDPR

Apply account controls to help protect access to the portal.

What SafeSite supports

  • Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords.
  • Administrative endpoints check the signed-in account's role before allowing reporter or plant-sector management.
  • Reporter accounts can be blocked, and login sessions use HttpOnly and SameSite cookie settings.

Current scope: These safeguards alone do not establish GDPR compliance. Privacy notices, lawful processing decisions, retention and deletion procedures, data-subject request handling, and a full security review are still needed.
